# Future ideas

Ideas we liked but deliberately left out of the first design. Each one needs its own discussion before it moves into the GDD.

## Captivity and ransom
Heroes who lose in war are captured instead of killed, and their realm, clan or family can pay a ransom to free them.
- Open problem: if nobody pays, captivity works like permadeath. It needs a fallback such as escape attempts, a maximum captivity time, or release on peace terms.

## Mortality and dynasties
Characters age and die, and an heir carries on the line. Parked as too heavy for now.

## Division captains
In army battles, players appointed by the general command one division within the general's orders. This would add a chain of command (marshals, constables) as political offices.

## Permanent item loss
Whether any gear can be destroyed for good (shattering), and which items are exempt (unique, legendary, relics). To be decided along with the durability system.
